Download & Installation Answers

Does CM RetailOS require an internet connection during setup or daily use? +
No. CM RetailOS is 100% offline-first. Installation is performed directly from the local installer package without connecting to any cloud server. Once installed, daily checkout, barcode scanning, shift reconciliations, and profit calculations run with 0ms local response even in basements or remote areas with zero network.
What is the difference between CM RetailOS and CM SheetViewer? +

CM RetailOS is the full retail operating system (POS, stock ledger, barcode scanner, profit tracking, cashier shift drawer management). CM SheetViewer is a dedicated standalone spreadsheet reader designed for shop owners and auditors to examine exported Excel reports (.xlsx/.xls/csv) without requiring a paid Microsoft Office license.

🔒 CM RetailOS Exclusive Monthly Reports: .CMP and .CMXL files are exported automatically by CM RetailOS and strictly require Admin credentials to decrypt and open in CM SheetViewer.
How does air-gapped licensing and activation work? +
CM RetailOS uses a secure cryptographic license file tied to your computer's unique hardware Device ID with an accessible fee of 5,000 UGX per month. You do not need to register an online account. Your license key unlocks full permanent offline access directly on your counter machine.
How does the Local Narrative Insights Engine work without an internet connection or cloud LLM? +
The Local Narrative Insights Engine runs 100% on-device directly within CM RetailOS. Instead of sending private financial records to cloud AI services, it uses fast, deterministic mathematical rules and statistical co-occurrence models over your local SQLite sales ledger. It transforms checkout numbers into clear executive directives: predicting peak trading hours across 9 diurnal windows (DEMAND PATTERN), flagging margin leaks where items sell below cost (MARGIN TO REVIEW / PROFIT DRIVER), and recommending bundle combinations (BASKET OPPORTUNITY). Everything runs locally with 0ms latency, zero API token costs, and complete privacy.
