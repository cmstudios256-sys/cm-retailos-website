The official web portal and download center for **CM RetailOS** — the modern hybrid retail operating system and Point of Sale (POS) engineered by **Christopher Muheki** and **CM Studios UG** in Kampala, Uganda.

---

## 🌐 Live Portals
- **Official Website**: [https://cmretailos.vercel.app](https://cmretailos.vercel.app)
- **Online Point of Sale (Mobile & Tablet)**: [https://cm-retailos-online.onrender.com](https://cm-retailos-online.onrender.com)
- **Windows Desktop Download**: [Download Page](https://cmretailos.vercel.app/download.html)

---

## 📱 Platform Availability
- **Smartphones (iOS & Android)**: ✅ Ready & available (instant zero-install PWA)
- **Tablets (iPadOS & Android Tablets)**: ✅ Ready & available
- **Windows PC (Desktop & Laptop)**: ✅ 100% offline-first Windows installer (`CM-RETAIL-OS-INSTALLER-v3.0.9.exe`)
- **Desktop Online Web**: ⏳ In active engineering

---

## 🛠 Tech Architecture
- **Core Engine**: [Vue 3](https://vuejs.org/) (SFC & SSR pre-rendering)
- **CSS Framework**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) + PostCSS
- **Bundler & Tooling**: [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **SEO & Discoverability**: Full SSG prerendering (`vue/server-renderer`), Open Graph, Twitter Cards, Schema.org JSON-LD Knowledge Graph
- **Hosting**: Vercel
