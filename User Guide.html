First-Time Setup & Onboarding Wizard

When launching CM RetailOS for the first time, an intuitive 4-step wizard personalizes your workspace theme, currency, store branding, and root administrator credentials.

Figure 1.1 Setup Wizard — Step 1: Color Scheme (Dark Mode, Light Mode, System Match) & 8-Color Accent Palette
How to read & use this screen

This is the very first screen CM RetailOS shows after installation — Step 1 of 4 of the onboarding wizard. The left rail tracks your progress through Theme & style → Business details → Admin account → Ready to launch, while the main panel asks you to choose your workspace look: a Color Scheme (Dark Mode, Light Mode or System Match) and an Accent Color from the 8-swatch palette.

  1. Pick a Color Scheme — Dark Mode is easiest on the eyes for night shifts, Light Mode suits bright sunlit shops, and System Match simply follows your Windows setting.
  2. Click any accent swatch to set the highlight color used on buttons, active menus and charts across the whole app.
  3. Press Continue to move on to Step 2.
💡 Pro tip: This choice is purely cosmetic — you can change the scheme and accent any time later under Settings → Appearance & Theme.
Figure 1.2 Step 1: High-Visibility Light Mode Active
How to read & use this screen

The same Step 1 with Light Mode selected so you can compare both looks before committing. Notice the two reassurances on this screen: “Select your preferred appearance and accent color. You can change this anytime.” and “Your details stay on this device.” — the wizard saves everything locally; no internet account is created.

  1. Toggle between Dark and Light and watch the entire wizard re-skin instantly.
  2. Judge readability from your real counter distance — sunlight near windows washes out dark themes.
  3. Confirm your choice and continue; the progress bar reads Step 1 of 4, 0% complete.
Figure 1.3 Step 1: Custom Amber Accent Selected
How to read & use this screen

Step 1 with a custom amber/orange accent selected over Dark Mode. The accent color is the single hue CM RetailOS uses for primary buttons, active sidebar items, links and chart highlights everywhere in the app.

  1. Click several swatches and watch the previewed controls change color.
  2. Choose an accent that clearly contrasts your scheme so actionable items stand out.
  3. Continue when you are happy with the combination.
💡 Pro tip: Match the accent to your shop signage or logo color for a branded, professional feel on every screen.
Figure 1.4 Step 2: Business Profile Form (Empty)
How to read & use this screen

Step 2 of 4 — “Tell us about your business.” The empty form waits for four required inputs: Country, Business or shop name, Phone number and Physical address. These details personalise the workspace and are printed on receipts and reports.

Hardware-Bound Activation & Air-Gapped Licensing

CM RetailOS uses a secure, cryptographic licensing system tied directly to your computer's motherboard and CPU hardware ID. Works 100% offline with zero cloud dependency.

Figure 2.1 Workspace Activation — Unique Hardware Device ID Extraction (DEV-7FCE-C62E-8A97-7E31) & Mobile Money Payment Channels
How to read & use this screen

The Workspace Activation screen. CM RetailOS has already extracted this computer's unique hardware fingerprint — Device ID DEV-7FCE-C62E-8A97-7E31 — and lists the OFFICIAL PAYMENT LINES (MTN & Airtel Mobile Money, account name Christopher Muheki). The two badges explain the design: Offline verification and Bound to this device.

  1. Click the copy button next to the Device ID to copy it exactly.
  2. Pay the licence fee (5,000 UGX per month) via MTN MoMo or Airtel Money to the official lines shown.
  3. WhatsApp your Device ID plus the payment reference to the vendor.
  4. When your activation key arrives, paste it into the XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX field with the Paste button and activate.
⚠️ Watch out: Licence keys are cryptographically bound to the Device ID that requested them. A key bought for another computer will be rejected here — always copy the ID from this exact screen.
Figure 2.2 Activation Key Validated (1C00-0JRX-9Q5J-CXDT)
How to read & use this screen

The same screen after pasting a purchased key, 1C00-0JRX-9Q5J-CXDT. The notice “New key: 1-month license – Starts when activated” tells you the countdown begins only when activation succeeds, not when you buy the key.

  1. Paste the 16-character key exactly as received — four groups of four characters.
  2. Press Activate; verification happens locally on the machine, no internet needed.
  3. On success the workspace unlocks and the 30-day period starts.
💡 Pro tip: Activate on the morning of a busy trading day so your first licensed day covers a full day of sales.
Figure 2.3 Settings > Activation: Active Workspace (30 Days Remaining)
How to read & use this screen

Settings → Activation, the in-app home of your licence. It repeats your Device ID, shows the activation key field, the remaining-days gauge (about 30 days on a fresh 1-month licence at 5,000 UGX/month, grace period noted) and the same official Mobile Money payment lines for renewals.

  1. Open Settings → Activation once a week and glance at the days remaining.
  2. Renew 2–3 days before expiry using the same Device ID flow so trading is never interrupted.
  3. After pasting a renewal key the countdown resets immediately.
⚠️ Watch out: When the licence lapses the workspace locks back to the activation screen — sales cannot be rung up until a valid key is entered, so never leave renewal to the last minute.

Workspace Authentication & Multi-Role Sign-In

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensures cashiers are locked strictly to checkout counters while administrators access financial profit ledgers.

Figure 3.1 Sign-In Gateway — Fast Role Switcher Tabs (Admin / Manager / Cashier)
How to read & use this screen

The sign-in gateway — “Welcome back to your workspace.” One window serves the whole team: role tabs (Admin / Manager / Cashier) sit above the Username and Password fields. The role you pick decides which menus and figures you will see after signing in.

  1. Tap the tab that matches your role.
  2. Enter your username and password exactly as created by the administrator.
  3. Press Sign In — admins land on the full dashboard, cashiers on the locked terminal.
Figure 3.2 Role Guard — "Account not found" Notice on Unprovisioned Manager Tab
How to read & use this screen

Role provisioning guard in action: when clicking the Manager tab before any Store Manager account has been created, CM RetailOS displays an informative guidance notice: “Account not found — Accounts for Store Managers must be created by an Administrator first.” The sign-in form is safely withheld until an administrator provisions the account.

  1. Sign in under the Admin tab first using your primary administrator credentials.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Users & Staff to add and configure a Store Manager profile.
  3. Once created, returning to the Manager tab will unlock the Manager username and password sign-in fields.
💡 Pro tip: Store Managers have elevated operational privileges (sales history, stock adjustments, and staff oversight), but system-wide master settings and root database tools remain strictly reserved for the Administrator.
Figure 3.3 Cashier Sign-In — Fast Username-Only Login (No Password or PIN Required)
How to read & use this screen

The dedicated Cashier sign-in mode branded “Secure access to your workspace”. Built for maximum counter efficiency: cashiers only need to enter their assigned Username to access their terminal. There are no passwords or PINs required for cashiers, enabling rapid lane handovers while keeping sensitive business data completely protected.

  1. Click the Cashier role tab.
  2. Type the cashier's assigned Username into the single input field.
  3. Press Sign In — the checkout terminal opens instantly, while cost prices, profit ledgers, and administrative settings remain strictly locked out.
  4. At the end of a shift, click the profile icon and select Switch Account so the next cashier can log in with their own username.
💡 Pro tip: Because cashiers have zero access to profit reports or system configurations, username-only login provides fast, frictionless shift handovers without compromising store security.
Figure 3.4 Header Profile Menu (Active User: Christopher Muheki, Admin)
How to read & use this screen

The header profile menu opened for Christopher Muheki (Administrator). It confirms who is currently signed in and their role, and offers Settings plus Switch Account without closing the app.

  1. Click your name at the top-right any time to verify the active user.
  2. Use Switch Account to hand the counter to another staff member cleanly.
  3. Admins can jump straight into Settings from this menu.
⚠️ Watch out: Never walk away from an unlocked admin session — switch account or sign out before leaving the desk.

Inventory, SKU Management & Android Scanner Bridge

Manage unlimited products, track stock thresholds, calculate automatic profit margins, and transform any Android phone into a high-speed camera scanner.

Figure 5.1 Category Initialization Prompt ("Create Your First Category")
How to read & use this screen

The Products page on a fresh install, blocked by the “Create Your First Category” prompt: “Before you can add products, you must create at least one category to organize and assign your products to.” CM RetailOS insists on categories first because they power the POS tiles, the filters and the profit-by-category charts.

  1. Click the prompt and create realistic shop aisles — e.g. Beverages, Bakery, Electronics, Fashion.
  2. Keep the list short and stable; you can always add more later.
  3. Once one category exists, the Add Product button unlocks.
Figure 5.2 Add New Product Modal — Auto SKU, EAN-13 Barcode Scan Field, Cost vs Selling Price with Live MARGIN, Min Stock & Unit Selector (pcs/kg/L/g/ml/pack/box)
How to read & use this screen

The Add New Product modal over the products table. Fields shown: Product Name (placeholder “eg. Fresh Orange Juice 1L”), an auto-generated SKU (SKU-991046), Category picker, Barcode “Scan or enter…” with EAN-13 format, Cost Price, Selling Price with an automatic MARGIN readout, Qty, Min Stock and the unit selector (pcs / kg / L / g / ml / pack / box).

  1. Type the product name exactly as it should print on receipts.
  2. Pick its category, then scan the barcode with your laser or Android scanner — or type the EAN-13 manually.
  3. Enter cost and selling prices; the margin badge instantly shows your profit per unit.
  4. Set opening Qty, a Min Stock threshold that will trigger Low Stock alerts, and the correct unit.
  5. Save — the product is immediately sellable at the POS.
💡 Pro tip: Set Min Stock to roughly one week of expected sales so the reorder alert arrives while the supplier can still deliver in time.
Figure 5.3 Add Product Image — "Browse PC" File Picker vs "Take Photo (Android)" USB Camera Scanner Bridge

The Add Product Image dialog with its two sources: “Browse PC — Select an image from this computer or a connected USB drive” and “Take Photo (Android) — Connect phone via USB with USB Debugging enabled.” The second option turns any Android phone into the shop's camera and barcode scanner.

  1. For Browse PC: pick any JPG/PNG from the computer or a USB stick.
  2. For Android: enable Developer Options → USB debugging on the phone, connect the USB cable, then choose Take Photo (Android).
  3. Frame the product or its barcode and capture — the image attaches to the SKU and shows on POS tiles.
💡 Pro tip: The Android bridge means you never need to buy a separate barcode laser — a used smartphone plus a USB cable covers scanning and product photos.

Frequently Asked Operations Questions

Quick answers to common daily checkout and management scenarios.

A customer changes their mind about an item after it was scanned. How do I remove it? +
Click on the line item in the cart and press the Delete key or the red trash icon. If your system is configured with Cashier Security Lock, an Admin or Manager must enter their PIN to authorize removing an item from an active cart to prevent theft.
What should the cashier do if the thermal receipt printer runs out of paper mid-sale? +
Don't worry! Complete the transaction normally. Open the printer lid, insert a new 58mm or 80mm paper roll (thermal side facing down), close the lid, and click the Reprint Receipt button in Receipts History. CM RetailOS will immediately reprint the receipt with all transaction details intact.
How do I give a discount to a customer without breaking profit reporting? +
Click the Discount field in the checkout summary. You can enter either a percentage (e.g. 5%) or a flat amount (e.g. 2,000 UGX). CM RetailOS automatically recalculates the final profit margin on that transaction and notes the discount on the customer's receipt and in the audit log.
Can cashiers see our supplier buying prices or our daily profit totals? +
No. In Cashier Mode, buying costs and store profit metrics are strictly hidden. Cashiers can only view retail selling prices, quantities, and their individual shift totals.
