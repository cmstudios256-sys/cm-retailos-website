How to Setup CM RetailOS Suite v3.0

Complete zero-cloud setup instructions for African retail shops, supermarkets, wholesale depots, and pharmacies. Get your PC, receipt printers, and barcode scanners fully operational in under 5 minutes.

100% Offline-First (0ms Latency) Windows 10 / 11 / 8.1 / 7 SP1 ESC/POS 58mm & 80mm Plug & Play Barcode Scanners Air-Gapped Device Licensing

System & Hardware Prerequisites

CM RetailOS is optimized for ultra-low hardware overhead. It runs fluidly even on entry-level budget counter PCs.

Computer Operating System

Windows 10, Windows 11, Windows 8.1, or Windows 7 SP1 (Both 64-bit and 32-bit architecture fully supported).

Memory & Processor

Minimum 2GB RAM (4GB+ recommended). Any dual-core Intel Core i3 / Celeron or AMD equivalent.

Local Storage

500MB free disk space for application files and high-performance local encrypted database store.

Counter Peripherals

Standard USB thermal printer (58mm or 80mm) and USB barcode scanner.

The 6 Steps to Full Store Readiness

Follow this visual guide to configure checkout lanes, print receipt tags, and establish cashier access rules.

01

Run the Setup Installers

Software Setup

Download and run the official installers from the download page on your Windows register computer. Both installers require no complicated database configuration, zero cloud accounts, and zero internet connection:

CM RetailOS Core Suite
CM-RETAIL-OS-INSTALLER.exe

Installs the primary desktop operating system for your checkout PC: POS checkout, barcode manager, inventory deductions, multi-staff access control, and shift profit accounting.

CM SheetViewer Auditor
CM-SheetViewer-Setup-1.0.0.exe

Installs the standalone spreadsheet companion allowing shop owners and managers to search, view, and audit exported Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV, and encrypted monthly reports (.CMP & .CMXL) without Microsoft Office.

Tip: During setup, make sure to check all options, including "Create Desktop Shortcuts", so cashiers can launch directly into the POS workstation every morning.
02

Connect Printers, & Scanners

Hardware Wiring

CM RetailOS communicates directly with standard retail peripherals over USB, Bluetooth, LAN, and serial interfaces:

Physical Counter Wiring Topology:
[ Windows PC / Laptop ] --(USB / BT / LAN)--> [ 58mm/80mm Thermal Printer ] --(USB Cable)-------> [ Laser Barcode Scanner ]

1. Thermal Receipt Printers (58mm & 80mm)

Connect your thermal printer via USB, Bluetooth / COM port, or Network TCP/IP. In CM RetailOS, navigate to Settings > Receipt & Printing, click + Add Printer, and select your protocol (Windows Spooler, Bluetooth / COM Port, Direct USB, or Network TCP/IP). Choose your roll width (58mm or 80mm) and click Test to verify print output and auto-cutter.

2. Laser Barcode Scanners

Connect any standard 1D or 2D USB barcode scanner. They operate automatically in USB HID keyboard emulation mode without requiring custom driver installation.

03

Configure Your Store Profile & Receipts

Branding & Currency

Open Settings > Business Information (under General & Branding) to customize the information that appears on your customer receipts and daily accounting ledger:

  • Business Name e.g., "Mutesasira Supermarket — Main Street Branch".
  • Currency Notation: Set to your local currency (e.g., UGX, KSh, TSh, RWF, SSP).
  • Receipt Header & Footer Notes: Customize return policies (e.g., "Goods once sold are returnable within 48 hours with receipt. Thank you for shopping with us!").
04

Load Products, Barcodes & Stock Levels

Inventory Setup

Populating your inventory catalog into CM RetailOS is simple and fast:

  • Open Products: In the sidebar under Inventory, click Products.
  • Click Manage: Click the green Manage ▾ button at the top right and select + Add Product.
  • Enter Item & Barcode: Fill in the Product Name, choose a Category, and scan the manufacturer barcode directly into the Barcode field with your scanner.
  • Set Pricing Margins: Enter your Cost Price and Selling Price. CM RetailOS automatically computes and tracks your profit per unit live.
  • Configure Stock & Units: Enter the Initial Qty, set a Min Stock threshold for low-stock alerts, select the unit (pcs, kg, L, g, ml, pack, box), and click Save Product.
Tip: Products added here are immediately ready in the Point of Sale screen for instant barcode checkout.
05

Create Staff Roles & Lock Cashier Mode

Cashier Anti-Shrinkage

Eliminate shop pilferage, unauthorized discounts, and cash leakage using granular permission controls:

  • Provision Staff Account: Navigate to Settings > Users & Staff (under Security & Access), click Add New Staff Account, enter the Username, and select the Role (e.g. Cashier).
  • Granular Module Permissions: Customize exact access rights across 6 dedicated permission toggles:
    • Point of Sale (POS): Checkout and sales terminal • Inventory Management: Products, stock levels, categories • Business Intelligence: Charts and profit analytics • Sales History: Past transactions and receipts • Audit Logs: System events and user audit trails • Settings & Configuration: Preferences and backups
  • Lock Cashier Mode: For counter staff, enable Point of Sale (POS) only and leave all other permissions unchecked. Buying prices, profit margins, inventory edits, and system settings remain completely inaccessible.
  • Save & Protect: Click + Create Account (or ✓ Save Changes) to instantly enforce role protection.
Anti-Shrinkage Protection: With locked cashier permissions, cashiers can quickly scan and ring up sales, but cannot view profit margins, alter past transaction records, or manipulate stock quantities.

Setup & Hardware Troubleshooting

Common setup questions and hardware troubleshooting solutions.

My thermal printer prints strange symbols or garbled characters. How do I fix this? +

This typically happens if the wrong connection protocol, baud rate, or paper column width is configured. In CM RetailOS, resolve this in a few simple steps:

  1. In the left sidebar, click Settings and open Receipt & Printing under General & Branding.
  2. Under POS Receipt Printers, click the Edit (pencil) icon next to your printer (or click + Add Printer).
  3. Verify your Connection Protocol / Transport:
    • Windows Spooler: Recommended for USB thermal printers. Click Scan to detect installed Windows printers, or enter the printer name (e.g., POS-80 or EPSON TM-T88VI).
    • Bluetooth / COM Port: For wireless Bluetooth or serial RS-232 printers (e.g., Bluetooth Serial (COM15)). Ensure the COM port matches Windows Device Manager.
    • Direct USB: For direct raw USB port printing without Windows spooler interference.
    • Network TCP/IP: For Ethernet or Wi-Fi POS receipt printers.
  4. Check your Paper Roll Width and Characters Per Line:
    • For 58mm rolls, choose 58mm (standard width: 32 cols).
    • For 80mm rolls, choose 80mm (Standard POS) (standard width: 48 cols).
  5. Click the Test button to verify clean character output and auto-cutting, then click Save Configurations.
Can I run CM RetailOS on multiple cash registers in the same store? +
Yes! Each checkout counter can run CM RetailOS independently on its own Windows PC.
What happens if my computer crashes or gets stolen? How do I restore data? +
CM RetailOS features an automated 1-click backup tool. You can set it to automatically create an encrypted backup of your entire sales and inventory database onto an external USB flash drive or external hard drive at the end of each day. You can restore this backup onto any new Windows computer in 30 seconds.
Do I need to buy Microsoft Office to view and audit sales reports? +

No! You can download CM SheetViewer directly from our download page — our custom standalone spreadsheet engine. You can open, filter, search, and audit all exported Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV sales reports with zero dependencies on Microsoft Office or internet cloud tools.

🔒 CM RetailOS Exclusive Monthly Reports
.CMP and .CMXL are encrypted proprietary formats exported automatically by CM RetailOS for monthly business reports. They strictly require Admin credentials to open in CM SheetViewer, ensuring that only the store owner or authorized administrator can access confidential financial ledgers.
