Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about setup, offline point-of-sale, hardware compatibility, and lifetime licensing for CM RetailOS.

What is CM RetailOS Suite v3.0.8 and who is it for? +
CM RetailOS is Windows software for handling sales, stock, receipts and reports in one place. It is designed for supermarkets, shops, pharmacies, wholesalers and other retailers, including businesses that need to work without a reliable internet connection.
Does CM RetailOS require an internet connection to work? +
No. Sales, stock and reports are stored on your Windows computer, so you can keep using the software when your internet connection is unavailable.
What hardware and printers are compatible with CM RetailOS? +
CM RetailOS is plug-and-play with standard, affordable retail hardware. It supports all 58mm and 80mm ESC/POS thermal receipt printers (USB/Network), laser barcode scanners, and touch monitors.
How does licensing and workspace activation work? +
CM RetailOS uses an air-gapped cryptographic licensing system bound to your computer's unique hardware Device ID with an affordable license fee of 5,000 UGX per month. Once activated with your license key, your workspace is fully unlocked with complete data sovereignty. All transaction data and profit ledgers stay securely encrypted on your local machine.
How are staff accounts and cash leakages controlled? +
The suite includes multi-tier Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Manager, Cashier). Cashiers can be locked into a dedicated POS checkout mode where cost prices, overall profit figures, and admin settings are completely hidden.
What sales reports are included? +
Reports can help you review busy sales periods, compare recorded selling prices with product costs, and find items that often appear on the same receipt. The examples on this page use sample data; your reports are based on the records entered in your own store.
