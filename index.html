Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about setup, offline point-of-sale, hardware compatibility, and lifetime licensing for CM RetailOS.

What is CM RetailOS Suite v3.0 and who is it for? +
CM RetailOS Suite v3.0 is a complete, 100% offline-first desktop retail management and profit intelligence platform built specifically for African businesses. It is engineered for supermarkets, retail duukas, wholesale distributors, pharmacies, hardware depots, electronics shops, and fashion boutiques. The suite gives you fast cashier checkouts, real-time inventory tracking, gross/net profit calculations, market basket analysis, and a standalone spreadsheet engine in one unified system.
Does CM RetailOS require an internet connection to work? +
No. CM RetailOS is 100% offline-first. Your entire point-of-sale terminal, barcode lookup, sales history, profit calculations, and inventory databases run locally on your Windows PC with zero cloud dependency and 0ms latency. Your business will never stop running due to internet outages, server downtime, or mobile data depletion.
What hardware and printers are compatible with CM RetailOS? +
CM RetailOS is plug-and-play with standard, affordable retail hardware. It supports all 58mm and 80mm ESC/POS thermal receipt printers (USB/Network), laser barcode scanners, and touch monitors.
How does licensing and workspace activation work? +
CM RetailOS uses an air-gapped cryptographic licensing system bound to your computer's unique hardware Device ID with an affordable license fee of 5,000 UGX per month. Once activated with your license key, your workspace is fully unlocked with complete data sovereignty. All transaction data and profit ledgers stay securely encrypted on your local machine.
How are staff accounts and cash leakages controlled? +
The suite includes multi-tier Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Manager, Cashier). Cashiers can be locked into a dedicated POS checkout mode where cost prices, overall profit figures, and admin settings are completely hidden.
What is the Local Narrative Insights Engine and how does it work without cloud AI? +
The Local Narrative Insights Engine is an on-device, deterministic intelligence system built directly into CM RetailOS. Instead of uploading your confidential transaction logs to cloud LLMs (like ChatGPT or Claude), it evaluates local SQLite ledgers with high-speed statistical algorithms right on your machine. It translates raw data into plain-English directives: predicting peak trading hours across 9 diurnal brackets (DEMAND PATTERN), flagging margin leaks where products are selling below cost (MARGIN TO REVIEW / PROFIT DRIVER), and uncovering cross-selling combos (BASKET OPPORTUNITY). Everything runs 100% offline with zero token fees and zero AI hallucinations.
